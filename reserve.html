How it works

From reservation to the cave.

  1. Reserve above. You get a confirmation email straight away, and a text from me if you asked for one.

Before you reserve

Why a pair?

CrossTalk needs two: one shoots the Frontsight, the other the Backsight, and they check each other. A single Tacheo still catches Backsights, FSx3 and loop errors on its own.

What does the warranty cover?

One year against defects in materials and workmanship in normal cave survey use, with free laser re-calibration.

Is the price final?

$380 per unit is the first-run price. Shipping is added when the units are ready to pay for.

Can I change my order?

Yes, any time before you pay. Reply to your confirmation email.
