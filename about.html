About

Built by a caver.

Tacheo, Topside and CartoTable are made by one person, Phillip Sullivan ("Sully"), for the way survey teams actually work underground.

Who

A project caver and cartographer.

I've been caving since 2018 and surveying on long-running projects in Kentucky, Virginia and West Virginia, with fifteen cave maps drafted. I've run survey projects and led expeditions, and spent more evenings than I'd like finding the one misread number that wouldn't let a loop close.

Tacheo started with that number. An instrument that knows the Backsight it's looking for, and two instruments that check each other before anyone leaves the station, catch the mistake while you can still fix it. Topside grew around keeping those instruments healthy, and CartoTable grew out of the drafting: the software side has been the hardest part of becoming a cave cartographer, and it shouldn't be.

Every Tacheo is assembled, calibrated and checked by hand before it ships. The software is free.

How it's made

Tested underground

Beta units are with surveyors on real projects, and their reports shape every firmware release.

Your data stays yours

Shots, calibrations and cave locations stay on your computer. Nothing is uploaded unless you press a button that says so.

Plain words

Screens say what a number means for the cave, not just the number.

Works with what you have

Walls, Compass, Survex and Therion in and out, and other makers' instruments read alongside your Tacheo.

Thanks

To the beta testers who've carried units underground and sent reports back. Laying sketches on the survey in CartoTable comes from CaveWhere by Philip Schuchardt. Declination uses the IGRF model; the geology lookup uses Macrostrat.

Development log

Built in the open, tested underground.

  1. Oct 2026First Tacheos built and in the hands of beta testers. Two-unit Frontsight/Backsight checking running in beta firmware.
  2. 15 Oct 2026The two-unit mode named CrossTalk, with FS/BS roles on the ready screen and ✓ / ★ agreement marks.
  3. 22 Oct 2026Reservations opened to the public for the first run.
  4. Nov 2026Tacheo 2 design settled: 2-inch colour screen, printed case, plug-in cells. Main board ready to order.
  5. 13 Nov 2026CartoTable named: the cartography and GIS program, with Reckon's blunder finder folded in. It opens from Topside's welcome screen.
  6. 14 Nov 2026Topside 1.6.77: CartoTable compiles a typed trip as soon as it's saved, and Home gets one tile per job.
