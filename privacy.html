Privacy

What goes where.

Short version: the website has no tracking, the forms go only to me, and your survey data stays on your computer.

This website

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs. The type comes from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google for the font files.

The Reserve and Message forms

What you type (name, email, what you're reserving, where it ships, your phone number if you asked for a text, and any notes) is sent to a script in my own Google account. It emails me, sends you a confirmation, and keeps reservations in a spreadsheet only I can see. I use it to handle your reservation or answer your message, and for nothing else. Your phone number is used only for the one confirmation text you asked for. Nothing is sold or shared.

Want your details changed or removed? Send a message from the Support page.

Downloads

The download buttons fetch the files from my Google Drive, so Google sees the request.

Tacheo

Tacheo checks for a newer version by reading one small file from Google Drive; it sends nothing about you or your instruments. A diagnosis report goes to me only when you press Send to Sully, with the name and contact you type in. Everything else (shots, calibrations, backups, logs) stays in Tacheo's folder on your computer.

CartoTable

CartoTable never uploads survey data or cave locations. Online map tiles, online elevation and the geology lookup are off until you turn them on, each with a warning that the server learns the area you're looking at.

The phone app

The Tacheo app for Android has no accounts, ads or analytics, and nothing is sold or shared. Your surveys, sketches and maps stay on the phone and go only where you send them: your own computer when you sync, or a file you choose to share.

Location. Used only when you tap to show where you are or to grab GPS coordinates. The position stays on the phone with what you save it to and is never sent to me. On older Android versions, the system also asks for location permission before the app can search for Bluetooth instruments; that search doesn't record where you are.

Camera. Used only to scan QR codes. The camera picture is read on the phone and isn't saved or sent anywhere.

Bluetooth. Used to connect to survey instruments you choose.

Online maps. Background maps come from Esri, the USGS National Map and OpenTopoMap. Like any map website, those servers see your IP address and the area you're looking at.

App log. Off unless you turn it on. It records which pages you open, Bluetooth connections and drops, errors, and your phone's model. It stays on the phone until you press send, then goes to me only, and I use it only to fix problems.

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. To have a log you sent deleted, send a message from the Support page. The app isn't directed at children.

The Tacheo itself

The instrument stores its shots and calibrations in its own memory. Bluetooth links only with units and apps you accept.
