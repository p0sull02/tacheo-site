Tacheo firmware and manuals

There's nothing separate to download: both come with Tacheo. Plug the Tacheo in with Tacheo open and use Updates and Upgrades → Tacheo; Tacheo keeps the right firmware for each kind of unit. The instruction manuals are on Tacheo's Manuals page.

Installing on Windows

Install in a minute.

  1. Unzip it. Right-click the downloaded zip → Extract All… and pick a folder you'll keep, such as Documents. Don't run it from inside the zip.
  2. Open Tacheo Topside.exe in that folder. Pin it to Start or the taskbar if you like.
  3. Plug in a Tacheo with a USB-C data cable, or click Cartography and GIS on the welcome screen to open CartoTable.
  4. That's the last download. Tacheo tells you when there's a new version and installs it with one click.

Mac

Tacheo for Mac is in beta.

  1. Unzip it (double-click) and drag Tacheo into Applications.
  2. Open it once by hand. Double-click it; when macOS says it can't verify it, click Done (not Move to Trash). Then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. On macOS 12–14, right-click it → Open → Open instead. After that it opens normally.
  3. Plug in a Tacheo with a USB-C data cable. On a Mac the CartoTable card opens Reckon, the blunder finder, until CartoTable comes to the Mac.

If Windows warns you

Why Windows might flag a new program.

Tacheo is a new program from a one-person shop, and Windows judges new programs partly by how many people have run them before.

"Windows protected your PC"

That's SmartScreen saying it hasn't seen this file often yet. Click More info, check the app is Tacheo Topside.exe, then Run anyway. It goes away as more people use each version.

Microsoft Defender says it found a threat

Defender's automatic (machine-learning) checks sometimes flag new, small programs, especially ones that update themselves and talk to USB devices, as Tacheo does. Each release is sent to Microsoft for review, and the flag usually clears within a few days.

Please don't switch Defender off or add exclusions. Instead, send me the file it named and the threat name it gave (Windows Security → Virus & threat protection → Protection history). I'll have it cleared and tell you when to download again.

Download only from this page (GitHub or the Google Drive links above), and keep Tacheo in the folder you extracted it to.

What's new

Recent releases.

1.6.113 · Topside is now called Tacheo: the program on your computer and the phone app are both Tacheo. The card for your units is Devices, and the units are the TD-1 and TD-2. Your settings, shots and caves stay where they are. Also new: MapViewer - your cave's drafted maps on the line plot; tap a quad and its newest map comes up. Windows offers the update by itself.

Phone app 1.10 · The Android app is called Tacheo on your phone. Nothing else changes.

1.6.110 · The survey file as a spreadsheet: edit shots in cells, front sights and backsights side by side, with each shot's row from your field book beside it. Profiles of any area you box on the plan (projected, extended or hybrid), a Statistics page with every survey's BCRA grade, a tie-in check, point clouds from cave scans placed on the survey, Will they connect? for two passages, and a Cartography page for map quadrants - who is drafting each and how far along, every quadrant exported at once. Drafted maps keep their transparency and are cut to their map frame.

Tacheo firmware 1.17.11 · Command and control: tap Cross section in the phone app and the unit in the surveyor's hand chimes and opens Cross section, ready to shoot. Tacheo offers it the next time a unit connects.

Phone app · Survey is one page: the units and their controls on top, then the survey shown like a page of your field book - a station a row, front sights and backsights, left/right/up/down - with arrows to turn the pages. Shots land on the page as they are taken.

Phone app 1.8 · GPS: your location on cave maps (with the passage under you and the nearest entrance), one-tap GPS points for ridge walking, sinks and new entrances, sent to your computer as GPX, CSV and KML. New surveys ask about the cave and take a GPS point at the entrance station, written into the survey files as its fix; when you're out, the Trip page closes the survey out - scan the book and sketches, and send it all to your computer in one go. How to.

1.6.109 · Data clinic: one page that finds everything wrong with a cave's data - blunders, bad loops, calibration errors, untied and separate caves, typos - shows each on a mini map, and fixes it one at a time (with undo) or all at once. It reads scanned field books too: checks the book's numbers against the survey files, finds leads marked in the book, shots written in the notes but never typed in, and writing on the sketch pages. Multi-cave projects are split into their own caves, and a Do everything button on Home runs the whole job - open, check, fix, map - and writes a summary PDF.

1.6.108 · New unit firmware now reaches Tacheo straight from the internet: Tacheo checks at start and when a unit connects, and says "New firmware for TCH-… Update it". Phone app: online base maps and layers (satellite, USGS topo and aerial, shaded relief, streams, roads, or your own) that can be saved for no signal, and Scan sketches and data - book pages captured like a document scanner, one PDF for your computer. How to.

1.6.105 · CartoTable projects are one file (.ctproj) with the data, sketches and map drafts in it, and Share Project writes it; declination from each trip's date with the most precise model (IGRF-14 plus the crustal field); find a cave's entrance on a lidar map and look up its elevation; cancel a cave while it opens. Updates on Windows now come from GitHub. Unit firmware: Tacheo 1.17.8 and SAP6 1.16.30.5 (messages from the phone).

Phone app 1.7 · Two or more Tacheos at once; one-way messages that pop up on a unit's screen; a Tacheo tile with each unit's battery and signal, and long range switched on by itself when the signal gets weak. How to.

Phone app 1.6 · Survey and sketch: shots from a Tacheo or SAP6 over Bluetooth (Android) or typed, legs and splays sorted as they come, the plan and extended profile drawn as you go and sketched in NSS symbols, the unit's battery, station names and cross sections, and Walls, Survex, Therion and SVG out. How to.

1.6.91 · CartoTable: a Layers chip on the plot turns station names, leads, blunder marks, lead search areas and each map layer on and off.

1.6.90 · CartoTable: Survey data opens on Books - each survey as a field book with its date, team, length, grade and leads; open one and the map goes to that trip.

1.6.89 · CartoTable: one viewer for the plot and 3-D, the 95 % uncertainty round a station, and trips graded by their loops and backsights.
