Topside

The Tacheo's companion program.

Updates, shot downloads, health checks and support for the instrument, plus CartoTable for the cartography. You don't need a Tacheo to use it.

Download TopsideCartoTable

At a glance

Plug it in. Topside does the rest.

Updates in one click

New firmware installs from inside Topside, and Topside updates itself. No downloads to hunt for, no files to copy.

Shots by survey day

Every shot grouped by trip, with CSV and Survex export.

Survey performance

Your survey speed and Frontsight/Backsight stats, trip by trip.

Reads other instruments

DistoX2, Disto XBLE, Cavway X1 and BRIC, alongside your Tacheo.

CartoTable

Cartography and GIS for the whole cave, one click from the welcome screen. Reckon's blunder finder now lives inside it.

What's inside

One program for every instrument on the trip.

Connect by cable or Bluetooth

Plug a Tacheo in with a USB-C data cable and it connects by itself, or add it by Bluetooth with no pairing. A pair of units can be connected at once.

Shots, by survey day

Download the shot memory and see it grouped by trip, with CSV and Survex export. Cross sections come out as a true-size field-book sheet.

Updates and upgrades

New Tacheo firmware installs in one click, and Topside tells you when there's a new Topside and updates itself. It only ever gives a unit its own build.

Diagnosis

A health check of a unit: battery, sensors, calibration and its own logs, written up in plain words. One button sends the report to me, so you don't have to describe the problem.

Every instrument it has seen

Each unit's history, calibration records and backups, kept on your computer. It also reads DistoX2, Disto XBLE, Cavway X1 and BRIC instruments (read only, in beta).

Manuals

The printable instruction manuals for the Tacheo and the instruments Topside works with, one click from the program.
