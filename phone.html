Free · Android and iPhone

Phone app

Your caves underground, with no signal.

The Topside phone app carries your caves from CartoTable. Once a cave is on the phone it works in the cave: where you are, the way out, how far it is, the leads nearest you and the trip books. Topside mode adds maps, trip planning and exports. And it surveys: shots from a Tacheo over Bluetooth, sketched as you go in NSS symbols.

What it does

Survey, sketch, and find your way.

Survey and sketch

Connect a Tacheo (Android) and shoot: three agreeing shots make a leg, the rest are splays, backsights and LRUDs are kept with their legs. The plan and extended profile draw themselves; sketch over them in NSS symbols - walls, steps, pits, water, formations, flow arrows. Saves Walls, Survex and Therion files and the sketches, and sends the trip to Topside on your computer.

Talks to the Tacheo

Fire a shot or the laser from the phone, read the unit's battery, calibration and trip, keep its station names in step with the phone's, and bring its cross sections into the sketch at their stations. No firmware change needed.

In the cave

A dark screen with big buttons. Pick the station you're at: how far in you are, the quickest way out and how long it takes, the distance between two stations, the leads nearest you, and the trip books to check an old survey on the spot.

Topside

The line plot in plan, profile and 3-D over imagery and the ground's shape; colour by travel time, depth, precision or trip; plan a trip by travel time, with the leads on the way; export GPX, KML, GeoJSON, CSV and pictures.

Data photos

Photograph book pages and sketches. They go to your computer, into a folder for the cave and trip, and CartoTable reads the data pages into survey data. Photos taken underground wait until there's signal.

My computer

Pair with Topside on your computer once and your caves come straight over. Run Check and Reckon there and see the findings, send files and notes, or use the computer's Topside from the phone. Over Wi-Fi, or anywhere with Tailscale.

Firmware updates, calibration and diagnostics still need Topside on a computer. Bluetooth to a Tacheo is in the Android app; on an iPhone the shots are typed and everything else works the same. How to survey and sketch.

Getting started

Four steps.

  1. Install it from the download page: the Android app, or the iPhone home-screen app.
  2. Pair it with Topside on your computer: Topside's first screen → Connect a phone, then scan its QR code from the app's My computer.
  3. Add a cave → Caves on your computer. It stays on the phone and works with no signal.
  4. Survey a trip: Home → Survey and sketch → New survey, tap 📡 to connect the Tacheo (Bluetooth → Phone On on the unit), and shoot.

No computer nearby?

Open a cave file.

CartoTable's Send to phone saves a .ctcave file to your Google Drive, OneDrive, iCloud or Dropbox. On the phone, Add a cave → Open a cave file. The full help and how-to covers every screen.
